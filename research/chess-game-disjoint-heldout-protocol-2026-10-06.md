# Game-disjoint chess explanation holdout — protocol v1 candidate

**Draft, 6 October 2026. No holdout game has been acquired, selected, opened, generated, or scored for this protocol.** This is an operational preregistration candidate, not a frozen experiment or an acceptance result. The [no-forward commentary gate](chess-commentary-capability-gate-v1.md) controls any capability claim. The [quality rubric draft](chess-explanation-quality-protocol-draft-2026-10-06.md) supplies the proposed targets below; the [standard-game cohort design](chess-standard-game-cohort-design-2026-10-06.md) is development work and cannot become this holdout by renaming it.

## Build a genuinely new set

Target **200 primary cases from 200 distinct games**, with 40 each for strong tactical, inferior, quiet/positional, defensive and endgame moves. Add **40 insufficient-evidence controls from 40 further distinct games**. These 240 are proposed targets, not observed denominators. The set is deliberately balanced and cannot estimate real-world move frequencies. Assign exactly one primary stratum before model output; record all overlapping tags. Use the development design's assignment priority `inferior → endgame → defensive → tactical → quiet` unless the freeze card explicitly replaces it.

Admit only complete, standard-start PGNs with a stable game ID, parseable result, error-free legal mainline, at least 16 plies, and the frozen time-control/ply bounds. Replay every move with pinned chess rules to derive the **pre-move** FEN, legal selected UCI and SAN. Keep result, clocks, comments, `%eval`, later moves and annotations evaluator-only. Count truncated PGNs, bad IDs, variants, illegal moves and every other rejection separately. A supplied PGN result is a completion declaration, not a proof of checkmate.

Before ranking candidates, exclude any game ID known to the project; an identical normalized UCI trajectory under another ID; and any canonical pre-move position already seen in development or protected material. Canonical position identity uses piece placement, side, castling rights and legal en-passant state, without move clocks. Also prohibit duplicate canonical positions and more than one case per game **within** the holdout. Query protected material through a count-only membership verifier; if that verifier cannot establish exclusions without exposing sealed cases, stop. General-model pretraining overlap cannot be proven absent and must remain an explicit limit.

Rank eligible games and candidate plies by SHA-256 of the frozen seed, namespaced game ID and ply, with lexical tie breaks. Fix the source byte cap, number of games/positions examined and engine-search cap before intake. Use board facts for provisional tags; a fixed, identical evaluator-side engine comparison may identify an inferior candidate (proposed ≥150 centipawn loss from the mover's perspective) or support a strong tactical candidate. Do not compare mate and centipawn scores numerically or treat bounds as exact. Recheck labels at a second frozen budget; keep failed, ambiguous and unstable candidates in the selection waterfall, without silently replacing them after the cap. A qualified reviewer checks quiet, defensive and endgame labels before outputs are revealed. These tags choose stress cases; they are not solution hints or proofs of a move's true merit.

| Primary stratum | Proposed pre-output selection rule |
| --- | --- |
| Inferior | Played move loses ≥150 exact centipawns against an unrestricted root under identical engine settings at both frozen budgets. Mate, bound or unstable comparisons are unresolved, not silently called inferior. |
| Endgame | No queens remain and at most six non-pawn, non-king pieces remain before the move; reviewer confirms the example teaches an endgame decision. |
| Defensive | The mover starts in check and legally answers it, or a separately specified legal-capture threat to a queen/rook is removed; reviewer confirms the defensive question is real. |
| Strong tactical | Played move captures, checks, promotes or creates a replayable tactic; the fixed evaluator does not find a material loss versus its root choice, and a reviewer confirms the tactic. “Strong” is a bounded study label. |
| Quiet/positional | Played move is no capture, check or promotion, mover was not in check, fixed evaluator finds no material loss, and reviewer confirms a useful positional question. |

The final freeze must turn “material loss” and any threat/tactic detector into exact code and numeric rules calibrated only on development games. If it cannot, remove that proposed stratum or stop before opening the source; do not decide from heldout outcomes.

Retain an auditable waterfall: source bytes and complete/partial games; each rights, header, replay and overlap exclusion; eligible games and positions; provisional and confirmed counts by all tags; engine attempts/failures/unstable scores; selected counts and shortfalls per primary stratum and controls. Seal a manifest of opaque case IDs, game IDs, pre-move position hashes, selection labels and source/engine receipts. If any target stratum is short, the target 240-case study is **not complete**. State the shortfall; do not change the source, seed or cap after seeing it.

## Generate once, then evaluate separately

For each sealed case, the generator may receive only the canonical, source-bound no-forward packet: pre-move FEN/side, selected legal move, deterministic transition facts, declared vocabulary and a typed observation **for that move** with source, engine, perspective, type and bound. It must not receive the primary stratum, another move or score, PV, post-move FEN, reply, later PGN play, puzzle/theme label, annotation or evaluator-only fact. Before any live call, a negative-control test must inject forbidden PV/post-move fields and prove rejection **before** dispatch. Hash and retain the exact permitted packet, full request, prompt, model/version/seed/settings and input audit for every scheduled attempt.

Use one frozen call policy per case. Record raw response bytes, observed model identity, timestamps and latency, parser result, timeout, transport failure, non-run reason or abstention as one attempt. Never turn free prose or a malformed response into a typed chess claim. Seal an append-only attempt manifest and its hashes **before** post-generation engine replay or human scoring. No failed request disappears from the denominator; no same-case retry is silently substituted. A later prompt or parser change starts a new experiment on fresh games.

The 40 controls test restraint on a particular unsupported **causal** claim, not silence about ordinary board facts. Their allowed packet must use a separately tested, versioned insufficient-observation shape; evaluator-only adjudication identifies what the input cannot establish. A model may state verifiable facts and a bounded hypothesis, but must not invent an exact Stockfish rationale, forced line or unsupported score comparison. If the request schema cannot express these controls without leaking their answer, controls remain unrun and acceptance is blocked.

## Blind scoring and prospective decision rules

After the raw seal, an independent checker legally replays every asserted move, capture, check, threat and displayed continuation from the source FEN. Compare engine claims with the exact typed observation and search conditions. Split outputs into board facts, engine observations, bounded strategic hypotheses and abstentions. Two reviewers, blind to model identity and hidden selection tags, independently rate usefulness from the pre-move board, selected move and text; then inspect a separate evaluator packet for factual adjudication. Keep both initial ratings, disagreement notes and the adjudicator's decision. Review all emitted claims, including controls and failures, not only favorable examples. An independent receipt reviewer checks the frozen manifest, no-forward audits, raw seals, code and complete denominators. If a qualified chess reviewer is unavailable, describe the result as a limited local candidate.

The following are **proposed thresholds to approve or revise on the freeze card**, not achieved results:

| Measure | Prospective rule and denominator |
| --- | --- |
| Legal and board accuracy | 100% of asserted checkable facts correct; zero illegal or invented lines. A malformed assertion is rejected or expressly abstained. Report asserted-fact and all-attempt denominators. |
| Engine/reference accuracy | 100% of asserted scores, perspectives, types and bounds exact or qualified; zero false Stockfish intent, forced-play or best-response attributions. |
| Strategic evidence | Zero contradicted categorical claims; at least 95% of nontrivial claims supported by replay/evidence or explicitly bounded, with claim counts. |
| Teaching value | Adjudicated 0–3 score at least 2 for **170/200 main cases overall** and **32/40 in each primary stratum**. Score 0 means wrong/misleading; 1 gives facts without a useful reason; 2 gives a useful reason and relevant choice with limits; 3 adds a clear causal contrast and practical lesson without overclaiming. |
| Restraint | No more than 10/200 full abstentions overall or 4/40 in any main stratum. At least 38/40 controls must withhold the unsupported claim, with zero invented facts. |
| Reliability | At least 95% valid typed outputs among **all 240 scheduled attempts**; report every parse, identity, transport, timeout, evaluator and not-run outcome. Report p50/p95 response latency with timeouts identified as censored, not omitted. |

Publish counts and 95% Wilson intervals overall and for each stratum; retain the per-case and per-claim rubric decisions. A case with any false categorical board/engine claim fails factuality regardless of its other claims. A missing, malformed or transport-failed main answer receives teaching score 0 and stays in every applicable denominator; a full abstention is also teaching score 0 on the main set. Meeting these numeric proposals alone does not confer professional quality: the independent no-forward review and qualified chess judgment in the capability gate must also pass.

The open [Chess.com practice game](chesscom-game-review-practice-2026-10-06.md) remains a development example. This holdout cannot establish that the system equals or exceeds Chess.com Game Review. Such a comparison needs a separate lawful, game-disjoint paired study with the same positions and learner question, source-preserved outputs from both systems, blind randomized ratings and a predeclared comparison including failures. Board-game expansion remains closed until the chess gate is independently accepted and a professional-quality claim is supported; each new game then needs its own rules, sources, legal adapter, evaluator and fresh review.
