helps a local chess-language model describe the board changes in its own line.
It follows a completed 192-update curriculum that improved narrow development
claims but has no completed matched result on fresh positions. The earlier
failed comparison's 16 games remain spent.

same 512 training source games, 32 development games, current-position inputs,
