# PlayGround Novelty Matrix

## Scope and interpretation
This is a primary-source comparison of the closest currently verified task families, not a claim that all neighboring work has been exhausted. `SoccerNet-v2`, `SoccerNet-Caption`, `SoccerNet-MVFoul`, `SoccerNet-GSR`, `TrajSV`, `SoccerLens`, `SoccerAgent/SoccerBench`, and `SVI-Bench` are soccer/sports-domain predecessors; `NExT-GQA`, `TVQA+`, and *Grounding Video Reasoning in Physical Signals* are near-boundary general-video benchmarks because they require answer-linked temporal evidence, spatio-temporal grounding, and a scored what/when/where box-trajectory output, respectively.

Status labels follow the evidence actually inspected. Nine projects have official inspectable repositories or documentation; TrajSV is verified from its immutable author paper and reports a deployed system, but no official implementation was verified. This matrix does not claim unrestricted media rights, current-stack reproducibility, legal open-source status, or independently audited production deployment.

The PlayGround row describes the **target research contract**. The implemented `playground-output-v1` schema is narrower: one unlabeled interval, string-array spatial evidence, and normalized broadcast-image trajectory points. Supporting-versus-contradicting labels, player relations, pitch-calibrated trajectories, and geometry-validity receipts remain proposed extensions.

## Defensible differentiation after this iteration

The broad statements “short-clip sports QA is novel,” “tool-assisted sports reasoning is novel,” “soccer multimodal QA or specialist-tool routing is novel,” “soccer event-classification grounding is novel,” “soccer pitch-coordinate reconstruction is novel,” “trajectory-informed sports-language generation is novel,” “video QA with temporal evidence is novel,” “spatio-temporally grounded VideoQA is novel,” and “one scored what/when/where answer with an image-plane box trajectory is novel” are **not defensible**. SVI-Bench directly precedes 10-second multi-sport Action QA and corpus-scale tool-assisted sports reasoning; SoccerAgent/SoccerBench precedes broad soccer QA and specialist-tool orchestration; SoccerLens scores event-class attribution against spatial cue boxes and cue-bearing frames; SoccerNet-GSR reconstructs athlete identity and 2D pitch position; TrajSV encodes broadcast-derived player/ball field-coordinate trajectories for downstream sports captioning; NExT-GQA requires answers and relevant video moments; TVQA+ links question/correct-answer concepts to boxes and evaluates answer-plus-temporal-span correctness; and the Physical Signals benchmark emits and scores one semantic/temporal/spatial object with box-trajectory references and visual perturbations. The narrower opportunity not found in these verified predecessors is their conjunction:

1. fine-grained 5–10 second **soccer** questions evaluated under an evidence-output contract rather than answer-only short sports QA;
2. answer-linked temporal **and soccer-spatial/trajectory** evidence;
3. supporting and contradicting evidence with explicit insufficiency reasons;
4. calibrated confidence, abstention, coverage, and selective-risk evaluation; and
5. a direct-vs-tool-augmented VLM comparison under the same evidence contract.

This is a working novelty hypothesis, not a completed literature-review conclusion.
