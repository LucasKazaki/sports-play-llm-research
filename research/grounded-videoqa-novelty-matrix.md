# Primary-source novelty matrix — grounded video QA boundary

Accessed 2026-08-06. This matrix compares unlike artifacts by claim-relevant capabilities rather than popularity. Reported metrics are author-reported unless noted; no model result below was independently reproduced in this project.

## Ranking by relevance to the north-star evaluation contract

1. **Explicit Abstention Knobs** — closest direct precedent for confidence-thresholded VideoQA under controlled evidence reduction, but without external grounding scores.
2. **TVQA+** — strongest direct precedent for jointly answering and exposing both temporal and spatial VideoQA evidence.
3. **Reliable VQA** — strongest open implementation precedent for selective multimodal QA, learned abstention, and risk-coverage evaluation, but image-only and ungrounded.
4. **NExT-GQA** — strongest direct precedent for human answer-conditioned temporal evidence and joint answer-grounding scoring.
5. **SoccerLens** — strongest soccer-specific attribution benchmark precedent, but not QA.
6. **TreeSoc** — strongest soccer-specific tool-orchestration precedent, but weak inspectability and no external evidence/uncertainty evaluation.

## Defensible novelty language after this comparison

Do **not** claim that PlayGround introduces grounded VideoQA, spatio-temporally grounded VideoQA, answer-linked temporal evidence, correct-answer-plus-temporal-overlap scoring, confidence-thresholded VideoQA abstention, frame-reduction uncertainty diagnostics, calibrated/selective multimodal QA, learned abstention, risk-coverage evaluation, grounded soccer understanding, or tool-augmented soccer QA. The remaining contribution hypothesis is the *combination* of short-play soccer QA with externally scored temporal evidence and **soccer-coordinate pitch regions/trajectories** (not generic image-plane boxes), with abstention and joint-grounded selective risk conditioned on answer-and-evidence validity, grouped leakage controls, and controlled answer-preserving evidence swaps/direct/retrieval/structured-tool/oracle/noisy-tool evaluations. This is a hypothesis pending a broader systematic search and a rights-cleared real-data pilot.

## Limitations

This is a targeted novelty matrix, not a systematic review. NExT-GQA is multi-choice, uses roughly 40-second videos with roughly 7-second evidence segments, and is not soccer-specific. TVQA+ is multiple-choice, television-domain, and box annotations sample one frame every two seconds within refined spans. Reliable VQA is image-only VQA v2 and evaluates answer selection without external evidence validity; its repository also discloses that an earlier evaluation used slightly different MMF reference answers, corrected by the v3 paper and standalone evaluator. Explicit Abstention Knobs studies one proprietary model on 300 NExT-QA questions; its frame-count intervention is not a ground-truth measure of semantic evidence loss, and no exact-title GitHub repository was found. “Working-open-source” means code and setup instructions were inspectable; it does not mean this project reproduced environments, data access, or results. No linked dataset, features, checkpoints, or video was downloaded.
