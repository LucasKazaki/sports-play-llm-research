# Protected-game exclusion: synthetic contract only

The prospective 240-case chess holdout needs to exclude any source game already used for training, prompts, practice, development, evaluation, or prior sealed work. The check must happen before ranking candidates and must not reveal protected cases to a generator or reviewer.

`scripts/chess_protected_exclusion_oracle_v1.py` implements a **synthetic-only** count check. It replays a full batch of legal games, normalizes Lichess game IDs and complete UCI trajectories, compares canonical pre-move positions, then publishes only exclusion counts. Its fixture used 240 distinct synthetic cases: 2 excluded by game ID, 1 by trajectory, 1 by position, and 236 eligible. The create-only r2 receipt is `artifacts/chess-protected-exclusion-oracle-v1/synthetic-20261006-r2/receipt.json` (SHA-256 `8bd855eab0cb00dbd15a7f554311086ccaa60a2c3aeb335c71b9d134bbcd969a`). Seventeen focused tests passed locally, and an independent source review passed the synthetic scope. A later repair made invalid protected-FEN errors omit the original FEN even from traceback context.

This does **not** verify real protected coverage. The source's batch completeness and registry coverage receipts are caller assertions, the one-query guard is process-local, and the private handle holds synthetic rows in memory. The current historical puzzle records do not establish every original game's full trajectory. A trusted registry owner, full original-game coverage, a durable one-query ledger, and a separately bound production receipt are required before the freeze-card validator can accept a real holdout. No real protected set was queried and no holdout was acquired, opened, ranked, or scored.
