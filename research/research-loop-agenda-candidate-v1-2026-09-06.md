## Five questions worth testing

1. **Does tool evidence improve joint answer-and-evidence correctness?** Compare the same base VLM, media, questions, output schema, and total inference budget with and without temporal/field tools. Primary endpoint: correct answer plus eligible temporal and field evidence on preregistered answerable requests; abstentions, timeouts, and malformed responses do not count as successes. Report each component separately.
2. **Does evidence-aware selection reduce unsupported answers at useful coverage?** Compare a validation-fixed self-score threshold with a selector using observable tool-validity features. Evaluate answer risk and joint-grounded risk versus coverage, plus false acceptance of unanswerable questions. A selector that answers nothing is not useful.
3. **Does the answer actually depend on the right evidence?** Use same-clip questions with distinct human cue intervals; compare intact inputs with irrelevant-frame removal, answer-destroying cue removal, shuffled time order, invalid calibration, and incorrect field evidence. Human annotators must label whether each perturbation preserves answerability. Changed answers alone do not prove causal faithfulness.
4. **Can a coach find the right evidence more reliably and quickly?** On a frozen corpus and independently judged queries, compare literal/FTS search, local query interpretation over the same index, and an evidence-enriched retriever. Include null, single, and multiple matches, semantic distractors, and paraphrases. Score moment relevance, null rejection, and correct evidence found within a fixed time budget; retrieval of an untrusted report is not a correct answer.
5. **When is another inference/tool call worth its cost?** Compare fixed single-pass and bounded selective verification policies, including a direct-model repeat-call baseline with equal budget. Report joint correctness, coverage, latency, peak memory, and total calls. This separates evidence benefits from simply spending more compute.

## The smallest defensible experiment sequence

**P0 — Prepare independently, while human/data gates remain open.** Freeze the query set, annotation manual, baseline interfaces, outcome schema, resource budget, and failure taxonomy. Audit historic-input hashes and whole-match provenance. Complete an official SoccerMaster encoder/head import and resource plan without acquiring weights or footage. Exercise derived-JSON query planning and faithfulness on existing permitted records; publish only systems outcomes from those cases.

**P1 — Establish annotation reliability before another model comparison.** Name two independent annotators and an adjudicator. Start with a training-only annotation pilot, including visible positives, background, near-miss events, replays, ambiguous camera views, and multiple questions per clip. Independently record answerability, accepted answers, minimal sufficient interval(s), live/replay status, image cues, and field regions/trajectories only where calibration is valid. Keep commentary hidden. Retain both original labels and adjudication reasons; report categorical agreement and disagreement, interval overlap, and coordinate error, including undefined/not-observable cases. Use a separate item reviewer and preserve the existing metric-eligibility controls. Models may draft worksheets but cannot stand in for independent human annotators.

**P2 — Run the already proposed 40-window, at-least-two-new-match diagnostic only after its existing authorization and overlap gates pass.** It tests failure mechanisms and implementation. Two independent match groups cannot support dependable population calibration, a broad generalization estimate, or a definitive superiority claim. Keep it separate from the larger QA/retrieval study and from all historical reruns.

**P3 — Preregister a confirmatory comparison on untouched matches.** Separate training, validation, and test by whole original match/source, keeping halves, overlapping clips, replays, and alternate encodes together. Document team, competition, season, camera, and resolution overlap; reserve a distinct domain-shift slice where feasible. Group splits do not prove absence of model pretraining contamination. Estimate required match count prospectively through clustered power/precision simulation using development-only variance and intramatch dependence. Record the minimum useful effect and desired interval width before selecting test size; if eligible independent groups cannot meet that design, retain diagnostic status.

Before test access, freeze one primary endpoint, hypotheses, sample size, exclusions, handling of abstentions and failures, calibration fit, operating thresholds, model/weight/runtime identity, randomness, hardware, annotation hashes, and analysis code. Amend [evaluation preregistration v1](evaluation-preregistration-v1.json) only through a new version; its existing thresholds and bootstrap choices remain intact for experiments that already use it. Seal predictions before unblinding labels; subsequent tuning consumes a new test set.

Use matched direct-VLM and tool-VLM baselines, temporal-only and field-only ablations, and invalid/shuffled tool evidence. Add an explicitly labeled oracle-evidence diagnostic to separate perception failure from reasoning failure; its privileged human evidence makes it ineligible as a deployable baseline. Hold input representation and sampling fixed for the main tool effect, then vary representation/sampling within each model. Compare models only after this confounding is visible. Treat commentary-only and visual-plus-commentary as separate arms.

Report paired effect estimates with match-clustered uncertainty, per-match results, all requested denominators, and counts of independent matches. Preserve fixed-threshold results and show risk–coverage curves; calibration needs a held-out calibration/validation set and uncertainty. Treat additional hypotheses as secondary with a declared multiplicity policy. On a tiny number of clusters, show descriptive results and label uncertainty unreliable rather than presenting a bootstrap interval as reassurance.

## Stop/go decisions and efficient loop behavior

- **Execution gate:** stop a lane on missing provenance, altered sealed inputs, unresolved label leakage, or a failed required validator. Record the exact failure and recovery; no silent fallback or success-only denominator.
- **Annotation gate:** no scored scientific claim until the independent roles, adjudication, and existing eligibility checks pass. Repeated systematic disagreement triggers manual/schema revision on development data and a fresh annotation pilot; majority vote does not make an unobservable event visible.
- **Benefit gate:** before the confirmatory run, choose a practically meaningful effect with the research owners. Declare evidence for improvement only if its prespecified confidence interval clears that threshold. A wide interval means inconclusive; a negative result remains reportable. Risk targets must be met with a prespecified minimum coverage and uncertainty bound.
- **Feasibility gate:** if the measured encoder/head cannot fit the approved local resource envelope, record that result and continue with the viable baseline. Do not silently substitute a smaller scaffold and call it SoccerMaster.
- **Compute gate:** permit at most one identical retry for a transient serving failure while retaining the first outcome; use materially distinct safe recovery strategies as required by the inherited autonomy policy before declaring exhaustion. Changes to method/configuration create a new diagnostic run. Bound each recovery packet and preserve failure receipts. Do not run an unrestricted prompt/model sweep on the holdout.
- **Operational efficiency:** keep at most three disjoint lanes: local experiments, primary-source radar, and coach/demo/document work. Each packet has one owner, a concrete question, input hashes, a time/call budget, a required artifact, and an acceptance check. Cache unchanged inputs and source checks; rerun only when an input or unresolved failure justifies it. The SoccerMaster watch records official-page/revision/license deltas at least every 24 hours when browsing is available; a no-change receipt is valid, duplicated essays are not.
- **Review and progress:** write versioned candidates, then independent Luna review, defect resolution, and Terra inspection of the exact revision and receipts. Track accepted artifacts, reproducible failures closed, annotation-ready items, stale lanes, elapsed time/calls per accepted packet, and review rework. Do not optimize for agent activity or document count. A Google Doc progress entry should distinguish running, failed, awaiting human/data input, reviewed result, and next experiment, with a timestamp and evidence link; operational status is not a promoted scientific conclusion.

The research program becomes stronger through falsifiable questions, independent labels, fair comparisons, reproducibility, and useful negative findings. Doctoral or publication merit still requires demonstrated contribution and expert assessment; it is not established by this agenda.
