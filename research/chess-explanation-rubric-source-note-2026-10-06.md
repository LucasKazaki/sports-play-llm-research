# Chess move explanation rubric: four practice moves and a later holdout

**Operator research note, 6 October 2026. Design only; no move has been scored here.** The four development cases are plies 33, 46, 57 and 93 from one known Chess.com game. The project's [four-case worksheet](chesscom-four-case-scoring-worksheet-v1-2026-10-06.md) remains unscored, and the [no-forward capability gate](chess-commentary-capability-gate-v1.md) controls any claim. The dated [Game Review observations](chesscom-review-breadth-observations-2026-10-06.md) are paraphrases without saved comparator screenshots or answer bytes. They are practice prompts, not model inputs or ground truth.

## What a good answer must do

Chess.com describes Game Review as combining a move classification, coach feedback, computer predictions, and a chance to retry a move. Its ordinary classifications use expected-points change; the review can change with deeper or different engine settings. These facts justify comparing *learner value on the same position*, not matching its label or displayed number to local Stockfish centipawns. [Game Review help](https://support.chess.com/en/articles/8584089-how-does-game-review-work), [classification guide, dated 9 February 2026](https://support.chess.com/en/articles/8572705-how-are-moves-classified-what-is-a-blunder-or-brilliant-etc), [classification-change guide](https://support.chess.com/en/articles/11845102-why-did-my-move-classification-change-in-game-review).

For a question of **why this move rather than that move**, the proposed key test is whether the reason identifies a verified difference between the two choices. Tim Miller's [published explanation review](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.artint.2018.07.007) explains why a property shared by the event and its alternative does not answer a contrastive why-question. This is an evaluation principle, not evidence that any particular chess idea is correct.

## Score each attempted case in two layers

**Layer 1: factual and provenance gate.** Bind the source game/ply, pre-move board, selected legal move, nine-field no-forward projection, raw request/response, model identity, settings, attempt, and timing. Check every asserted square, piece, attack, capture, check, legal alternative, reply, and material consequence by legal replay. A claimed engine observation must retain engine/version, root, search setting, side perspective, centipawn versus mate type, and lower/upper-bound flag. A numeric move gap requires the played move and **model-named** alternative in the same pinned root-restricted paired search, both with finite unbounded centipawn observations from the same perspective; otherwise say that the ranking is unresolved. Stockfish documents [`searchmoves`, node limits, `MultiPV`, and score/bound output](https://official-stockfish.github.io/docs/stockfish-wiki/UCI-Protocol-and-Stockfish-Commands.html). A search line is one engine continuation, not proof that a human rationale is forced. A false categorical fact, invented line, false engine attribution, or missing no-forward audit is `F-fail` and stays in the denominator. A truthful abstention on an unsupported claim passes the factual gate but does not automatically teach the move.

**Layer 2: teaching score, only after factual passage.** Use the project's existing 0–3 scale. `0`: empty, full abstention on an answerable case, or a vague/shared-fact reason. `1`: correct fact or qualified score without a practical consequence. `2`: a specific legally checked conditional mechanism and useful choice, with uncertainty where ranking remains unresolved. `3`: a verified *discriminating* contrast with the model-named alternative and a plain-language decision a learner can reuse; a score gap alone cannot earn 3. `F-fail` contributes 0 to summary arithmetic but is reported separately. An unsupported forcedness or “Stockfish thinks this because ...” claim fails regardless of fluent prose. Retain atomic claims, evidence paths, both judges' original ratings, and adjudication. This aligns with the [existing project rubric](chess-explanation-comparison-rubric-2026-10-06.md).

Report over **all four scheduled attempts**, including not-run, interrupted, timeout, malformed, transport failure, checker rejection, and abstention. Keep separate rates for (a) factual pass, (b) verified causal contrast, (c) appropriate restraint when evidence is insufficient, (d) unnecessary full abstention on answerable cases, (e) teaching score, and (f) latency p50/p95 plus failures. Do not repair an answer after seeing evaluator-only evidence and count it as the original answer. The four cases can expose defects; they cannot establish parity, all-move coverage, or professional chess teaching.

## Separate game-disjoint holdout

Use the [project's draft quality protocol](chess-explanation-quality-protocol-draft-2026-10-06.md) as a **proposal**, not a completed preregistration: 200 positions from 200 distinct permitted games, with 40 primary cases each for strong tactical, inferior, quiet/positional, defensive, and endgame moves, plus 40 separately frozen insufficient-evidence controls. Exclude this practice game and every source game used for prompt development, historical examples, or the existing cohort. Freeze selection, source rights/hashes, duplicate checks, learner questions, generator packet allowlist, model/settings, engine budget, thresholds, reviewers, and analysis before opening any holdout output. Preserve every scheduled case and missing/failing attempt. The controls should test whether the system withholds only the unsupported assertion while still stating safe board facts.

Two model-identity-blind readers should rate the same position and learner question, recording initial scores and adjudication; at least one qualified chess reviewer is required before a professional-level claim. Report factuality, engine-reference validity, strategic support, teaching, abstention, latency/failure, and uncertainty overall and by stratum. An independent receipt review must verify the no-forward negative control and the raw denominator. For a **Chess.com head-to-head** claim, first retain lawful, timestamped comparator outputs and review settings on those same new positions, randomize source-hidden paired presentations, and predeclare a comparison including failures. Today's unsaved UI paraphrases cannot be those comparator outputs. Until the no-forward gate and qualified judgment pass, the defensible label is a local evidence-grounded commentary candidate; board games and human sports require separate evidence.
