# Archit/Coach Demo Evidence Boundary Repair Candidate

## Evidence Admissibility
- Only publicly available, open-source, or openly licensed media and facts are admissible.
- All evidence must preserve claim-level provenance and be traceable to its source.
- Synthetic results and real-data results must be explicitly separated in all outputs.

## Retrieval and Provenance Display Requirements
- All retrieval results must include a clear provenance statement indicating the source and date of the data.
- Provenance must be displayed in a standardized format (e.g., `Source: [URL] | Date: [Date]`)
- Retrieval results must be presented in a structured, machine-readable format (e.g., JSON or YAML) alongside human-readable summaries.

## Abstention Behavior
- When evidence is insufficient, ambiguous, or conflicting, the system must abstain from making claims.
- Abstention must be explicitly stated with a clear rationale.
- The system must not infer or extrapolate beyond available evidence.
