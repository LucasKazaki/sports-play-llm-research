# Prospective holdout freeze-card validator — 6 October 2026

The [game-disjoint study plan](chess-game-disjoint-heldout-protocol-2026-10-06.md) is still a candidate. No holdout game has been opened, selected or scored. The new offline `scripts/chess_holdout_freeze_card_v1.py` checks whether a proposed freeze card has the required structure and consistent pins **before** anyone could treat it as a study configuration. It deliberately cannot approve a study or establish that any self-reported source, rights, exclusion or reviewer fact is true.

The validator requires the exact standard-rated archive URL and calendar month, role-specific Lichess metadata URLs, source/broker byte caps, development-month and archive separation, code and model pins, a count-only protected-exclusion oracle receipt, a no-forward negative-audit receipt with zero model calls, deterministic 200+40 sampling rules, two engine budgets, 240 one-attempt outcomes with raw seals, independent reviewer roles, and prospective thresholds. Every receipt is pinned by the bytes of its JSON file. Its only successful status is `prospective_structural_candidate_only`, with `holdout_frozen=false`.
