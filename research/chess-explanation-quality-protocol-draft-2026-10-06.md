# Chess explanation quality: proposed evaluation protocol

**Draft for review, 6 October 2026. No cases have been selected or scored under this protocol.** Freeze a numbered successor with exact source hashes, selection rules, prompts, model settings, reviewers, thresholds, and analysis code *before* opening a fresh holdout. The [no-forward capability gate](chess-commentary-capability-gate-v1.md) remains the acceptance authority.

## The question we should test

Can the local model give a learner a short, accurate reason for a move, explain a sound alternative when it has evidence, and admit what the position and engine observation do not establish? This needs five kinds of moves: strong tactical choices, inferior moves, quiet positional choices, defensive resources, and endgames. A score or a legal line alone is not an explanation. For example, the [Chess.com practice game](chesscom-game-review-practice-2026-10-06.md) showed why “the pawn can be captured” was an incomplete reason for preferring 20.Qe4 over 20.Qc3: the same pawn exchange is legal after both moves, while the options available afterward differ. That game and the existing development pages are useful for improving the rubric; they are **not** blind test cases.

## Proposed blind set and boundary

- Select **200 positions from 200 distinct, permitted source games**: 40 primary cases in each of the five move types above. Allow secondary tags, but assign one primary stratum per case before inference. Within each stratum, include both sides to move, different game phases where applicable, and more than one source slice. Record the full candidate denominator, exclusions, rights, source bytes and hashes, legal replay, duplicate-game and duplicate-position checks, and sampling seed. Exclude every game used in project-controlled training or prompt development, the historical eight-case examples, the existing 24-position cohort, and the Chess.com practice game. General-model pretraining overlap may be unknowable and must be named as a limitation. Do not inspect the existing sealed-heldout case details while designing this protocol.
- Define the move-type selector and any engine thresholds on a **separate development sample**, then freeze them. “Inferior” must mean a verified evaluator-side contrast under the same predeclared engine setting, with score perspective, type and bounds respected; a Chess.com label or the difference between two unrelated searches cannot define it. Have a qualified reviewer audit a stratified selection sample for mislabeled quiet, defensive, and endgame cases before model output is revealed. Retain all sampled cases, including ambiguous ones and failed requests, in the declared denominator.
- Give the generator only the source-bound pre-move FEN, side, selected legal move, deterministic transition facts, declared concepts, and the permitted **typed observation for that move**. Do not pass its primary stratum, an alternative move or score, PV, post-move FEN, future play, puzzle solution/theme, annotation, Chess.com comment, or evaluator-only fact. Freeze the canonical packet and hash, full actual request, model/version/seed/settings, prompt, response, timing, and error for every attempt. The before-call checker must reject unknown and forbidden fields. Retain a negative-control test in which PV and post-move fields are deliberately inserted and the model is never called.
- Keep all alternative-move search, legal reply replay, claim checking, and engine comparison **after generation** in a separate evaluator. Its observations may verify or falsify the model's text, but may not be sent back into the same output. If a new prompt or adapter is needed after a failure, version it and use a fresh, predeclared study; do not patch a frozen run or repeat an identical failed call.

## Score the explanation, not just the move

For each output, split every sentence into checkable assertions and retain its claimed type: replayed board fact, exact qualified engine observation, bounded strategic hypothesis, or abstention. A parser rejection, empty answer, transport error, timeout, and not-run request each remain visible in the denominator. Report counts and 95% Wilson intervals overall and by primary stratum; do not hide weak strata in an average.

| Measure | What a reviewer checks | Proposed pass rule for the frozen successor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Legal and board facts | Every named square, piece, capture, check, legal alternative and stated continuation is replayable from the source position. | **100%** of asserted checkable facts correct; zero illegal or invented lines. A malformed assertion is rejected or expressly abstained. |
| Engine-reference validity | Scores keep the recorded side, centipawn/mate type, bound and search conditions. No score is presented as a human rationale, forced line, or directly comparable to another search without a common setting. | **100%** exact or qualified; zero false Stockfish attributions. |
| Strategic evidence | Each causal claim names a board fact, a legal tested variation, or a clearly qualified observation. Plausible but untested ideas are labelled hypotheses; categorical claims such as “wins a pawn” require replay. | Zero contradicted categorical claims; at least **95%** of nontrivial strategic claims are supported or explicitly bounded, with numerator and denominator published. |
| Teaching value | Two blind readers score 0–3: 0 = wrong/misleading; 1 = accurate facts without a useful reason; 2 = useful reason and relevant choice, with a clear limit; 3 = clear causal contrast, useful alternative, and practical lesson without overclaiming. | The adjudicated score is **2+ for at least 170/200 overall and 32/40 in every stratum**. Record both initial scores and every disagreement. |
| Appropriate restraint | An answer should not fill missing evidence with a made-up tactic; withholding everything on an ordinary clear case also fails to teach. | At most **10/200** full abstentions and at most **4/40** per stratum on the main set, with each abstention reviewed for cause. Add **40 source-bound insufficient-evidence controls**, frozen separately, and require at least **38/40** to abstain from the unsupported *claim* with zero invented facts. |
| Reliability and speed | Measure start-to-raw-response latency and all parse, transport, model and evaluator failures. | Report p50/p95 latency, complete failure counts and rate on **all 240 scheduled attempts**. Proposed product threshold: at least **95%** valid returned outputs, with no missing case silently dropped. |

The 40 controls must use legal, source-bound positions and a versioned permitted request shape. A bound or weak observation can make a particular causal assertion unanswerable while basic board facts remain answerable. Label the *unsupported claim* in evaluator-only data; do not put its answer in the generator packet. If the current schema cannot express such a request safely, implement and test a new version before freezing. The counts and pass rules above are **proposals**, not retroactive acceptance criteria for any existing capture. The successor protocol should also specify how multiple assertions per case are aggregated and how a censored timeout enters latency summaries.

## Independent judgment and comparison

Use two reviewers who did not write prompts, inspect the hidden selection labels, or see model identity while rating. At least one should be a qualified chess teacher or player whose qualification is recorded. Give them the pre-move board, selected move, model text, and a separate evaluator packet with legal replays and typed engine evidence; reveal the model identity only after scores are locked. Resolve disagreements with a recorded adjudication and keep both initial ratings. An independent receipt-based reviewer then checks the frozen protocol, every input audit, raw outputs, score code and denominators. If a qualified chess reviewer is unavailable, use a documented limited substitute and claim only a local evidence-grounded candidate, not professional quality.

The open Chess.com Game Review is a **practice and interface reference**. It cannot establish parity or superiority from one example or from engine numbers gathered under different settings. A later head-to-head claim would need a separately approved, lawful, game-disjoint paired sample; the same positions and learner question; source-preserved outputs from both systems; blind, randomized ratings for factual soundness and teaching value; and a predeclared statistical comparison that includes failures. Do not use Chess.com commentary as generator input or a hidden answer key.

## Decision and next step

Passing software tests or the numeric proposal above is not, by itself, a professional-quality verdict. The [capability gate](chess-commentary-capability-gate-v1.md) requires independent acceptance of the no-forward audit and qualified chess judgment. Only after that gate and a supported professional-quality claim may the director open a **game-specific** board-game packet: authoritative rules and source rights, legal-state adapter, evaluator, fresh heldout and human review for each game. Human sports needs separate evidence and evaluation, not a chess transfer claim.

Next, review this draft against the current typed-output transport and actual development failures, agree on the qualified reviewer and feasible sample budget, then freeze a numbered protocol before touching new heldout outcomes. The current local paired practice page and model-transport work remain development evidence only.
