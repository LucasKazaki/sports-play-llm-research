# Writing and interface method — candidate working note

Lucas requested a review of AI-writing research before tonight's sports work. This note records the sources consulted and the limits of applying them. It does not promise detector avoidance or misrepresent authorship.

[StoryScope, Russell et al. (2026), version 6](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03136v6) studies long fiction, using features such as character agency and the ordering of events. Its results suggest that structural choices can distinguish the sampled model stories from human stories. They do not establish a classifier for engineering notes or a method for making research reports undetectable.

[Kobak et al. (2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.07016v5) study vocabulary shifts across biomedical abstracts. Their aggregate estimates concern a corpus and an assumed pre-LLM baseline. They should not be treated as proof of authorship for one passage or as a list of forbidden words.

For this project, the useful editorial choices are ordinary research practice: start with the question or observed failure, show the measured denominator, distinguish observations from interpretation, and keep limitations beside the affected claim. Do not invent personal experience, a stronger result, or a tidy success story. Lucas should review candidate notes and supply his own judgment before using them with his supervisor. The installed Apple Design skill supplies the interface direction: a visible current task, readable type, deliberate spacing, keyboard access, user control over imported evidence, and restrained visual treatment. The chess board and retained evidence should carry the page. An imported model response must remain distinguishable from a verified board fact.

For an additional web accessibility reference, [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/quickref/) supplies testable contrast, keyboard, focus and reflow criteria. We consulted [Material Design's accessibility overview](https://m3.material.io/foundations/accessible-design/overview) as a second design system, but it yielded no readable guideline content through the available page fetch; no specific rule is attributed to it. No extra design plugin or skill was installed.

These sources guide clarity and usability. They do not verify chess understanding, scientific novelty, or compliance with a supervisor's rules about AI assistance.
