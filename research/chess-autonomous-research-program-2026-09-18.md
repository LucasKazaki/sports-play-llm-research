## Current empirical starting point

`data/open/chess/lichess-real-seed-v1/manifest.json` binds 24 puzzles derived from
24 real Lichess games to retained upstream bytes, acquisition metadata, the
provider's CC0 declaration, and 122 legally replayed plies. Eight cases each
belong to train, development and test. The intake takes at most 262,144 compressed
bytes; it does not download the complete database. The test split has had only
legality and integrity checks, with no engine/model scoring.

The development baseline runs Stockfish 19 locally with one CPU thread, 16 MB
hash and fixed 10,000/100,000-node budgets. Both conditions matched the published
solution on 8/8 development cases. This tiny first-rows convenience sample is
easy for this engine and does not measure explanation quality, teaching value,
general chess ability, or sports transfer. Its value is a real, rerunnable source
and executable baseline from which to expose harder questions. Preserve all
failed/not-run requests in the original denominator. Mate distance and centipawn
evaluation are distinct types. Score perspective is always explicit.

The source convention matters: the FEN precedes the opponent's setup move; the
second published move starts the solution. Puzzle themes are automatic labels,
not human commentary. Neither themes nor the solution enter the engine input.
No model was called by this baseline. Deterministic descriptions state checked
board facts and are never labelled learned explanations.

## Useful next frontier

- Ground counterfactual explanations: engine candidate alternatives, legal
  transition facts, source-linked claims, and precisely stated uncertainty.
- Detect explanation errors on real development boards: impossible captures,
  incorrect checks, illegal variations, reversed score perspective, fabricated
  evidence references and unsupported strategic claims.
- Compare the discovered configured local generator with deterministic facts
  and raw-PV baselines on the same frozen development inputs. Measure factuality,
  evidence coverage, abstention, latency and failure rate; defer human teaching
  ratings without turning their absence into a coding blocker.

  move, alternatives, claim evidence and limitations. Preserve existing services;

  held-out games. Build a fresh evaluation protocol before spending the holdout.
