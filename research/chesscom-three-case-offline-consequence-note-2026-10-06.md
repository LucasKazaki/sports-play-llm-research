## Ply 57 — 29.Rac1 and the observed 29.Rb1 option

This difference has a concrete conditional consequence: after either first move, `47...b1=Q` is legal, but **only after Rb8** does White have the immediate legal `48.Rxb1`, capturing the newly promoted queen from b8.
